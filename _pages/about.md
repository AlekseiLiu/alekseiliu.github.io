PhD in Machine Learning, dissertation submitted September 2026, defence expected December 2026, in the [Machine Learning group (HammerLab)](https://hammer-lab.techfak.uni-bielefeld.de/) at Bielefeld University. My research spans two areas: (1) **generative AI for neural architecture search** - DGPO, an RL-steered graph diffusion model that matches the NAS-Bench-201 optima on all three tasks (by benchmark lookup) and, pretrained on only 7% of NAS-Bench-101, produces a best generated architecture over evaluation draws within 0.32 pp of the full-data model (accepted at IJCNN 2026); and (2) **robust anomaly detection on dynamic graphs** - from interpretable AutoML pipelines to temporal graph neural networks with probabilistic uncertainty modeling. Published 6 papers (5 first-author) in IJCNN, ICANN, ESANN, IDEAL, and Applied Soft Computing.
